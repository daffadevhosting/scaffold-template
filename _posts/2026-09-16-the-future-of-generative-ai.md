# The Future of Generative AI

Generative Artificial Intelligence is reshaping the landscape of creativity. From generating photorealistic images to composing complex symphonies, the boundaries between human and machine-assisted art are blurring.

## Key Technologies

* **Large Language Models (LLMs)**: The engine behind conversational AI.
* **Diffusion Models**: The technology driving high-quality image generation.
* **GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks)**: Used for creating realistic synthetic data.

## Ethical Considerations

As we move forward, we must address critical questions regarding:
1. **Copyright**: Who owns an image generated by a prompt?
2. **Bias**: How do we prevent AI from amplifying existing societal prejudices?
3. **Job Displacement**: How will creative professionals adapt to this new toolset?
